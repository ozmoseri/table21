Coins are fictional: no purchases, cash value, redemption or transfers. Never change rules or odds to raise usage or
purchases. No fake players, no loss shown as a win, no pressure to buy after a loss, no punitive streak loss.
Prefer friends, game nights, cosmetic progress, fair challenges and a sense of improvement. Separate the current scope
from future proposals; store, wheel, scratch and battle pass are future items with dependencies and acceptance tests.
Do not add RevenueCat, Firebase or similar without a demonstrated need.
